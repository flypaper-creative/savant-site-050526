# Savant Logo 3D Direction

- Extrude the primary glyph as bevelled black chrome or dark graphite.
- Use shallow bevels, hard specular edges, and no generic circuit texture.
- Animate the upper prism and lower seal as two magnetically aligned glyph plates.
- During preload, percentage progress can be represented by internal edge illumination traveling through the glyph seams.
- Final frame: glyph centered, wordmark optional, logo fills viewport with roughly 20px side padding.
